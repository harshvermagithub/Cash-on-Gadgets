5.0 Probationary Period and Formal Confirmation

(a) Probationary Term: You shall serve a probationary period of 45 (Forty-Five) Days commencing from your effective Date of Joining. The purpose of this period is to provide both you and the Company an opportunity to assess mutual suitability, technical alignment, and performance standards.

(b) Performance Assessment: During the 45-day probationary period, your engineering output, code test coverage, sprint velocity, problem-solving aptitude, communication, and adherence to company architectural standards will be evaluated.

(c) Notice Period During Probation: During this 45-day probationary period, either party may terminate the employment relationship by serving 7 (seven) calendar days' prior written notice or gross basic salary in lieu thereof, without assigning any cause.

(d) Confirmation of Services: Upon satisfactory completion of the 45-day probationary term, the Company shall issue a formal written Confirmation Letter. Unless and until confirmed in writing by authorized executive management, you shall continue to be deemed on probationary status.

6.0 Working Hours, Operating Schedule and Leave Policy

(a) Operational Schedule: The standard work schedule comprises a 5-day working week (Monday through Friday), with standard business hours between 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Given the dynamic nature of software engineering, you may be called upon to participate in scheduled release windows or resolve critical production incidents outside normal hours.

(b) Leave Entitlements: Upon confirmation, you shall be entitled to paid annual leaves, casual leaves, and national holidays in accordance with the Company’s official HR Leave Policy. All leaves must be planned in advance with sprint leads to prevent project bottlenecks.

7.0 Scope of Engineering Duties and Performance Standards

As a Software Development Engineer, your mandate encompasses the architectural design, implementation, testing, maintenance, and deployment of scalable software applications, internal operational tooling, customer portals, automated device valuation algorithms, order processing pipelines, and partner APIs powering FonzKart's re-commerce ecosystem.

  • Architect, write, review, and maintain modular, clean, and testable code across full-stack applications.
  • Participate actively in sprint planning, agile standups, system architectural designs, and automated testing cycles.
  • Integrate secure third-party APIs, including payment gateways, SMS/WhatsApp communications, and logistics partner webhooks.
  • Diagnose complex software bottlenecks and database performance queries to maintain 99.9% platform reliability.
  • Implement defensive programming, rigorous input sanitization, and industry-standard security protocols across all endpoints.
