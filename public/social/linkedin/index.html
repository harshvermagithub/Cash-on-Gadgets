3. Step-by-Step Creation Walkthrough on LinkedIn

  1. Log in to your LinkedIn account on your computer at linkedin.com.
  2. In the top right navigation bar, click the For Business grid icon (or Work icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the popup menu and click Create a Company Page +.
  4. Select Company (Small, medium, and large businesses).
  5. Fill in the Company Details using the copy buttons above (Name: Fonzkart, Website: https://fonzkart.in, Industry: Retail / Re-commerce, etc.).
  6. Upload the Profile Logo (fonzkart_linkedin_profile_logo_400x400.png) downloaded above.
  7. Check the verification box ("I verify that I am an authorized representative of this organization...") and click Create Page.
  8. Once the page opens, click the edit pen icon on the cover image and upload the Cover Banner (fonzkart_linkedin_cover_banner_1128x191.png).
  9. Add the About Us description, Specialties, and Location from Section 2.
  10. Click Publish! Your official Fonzkart LinkedIn Company Page is now live!
